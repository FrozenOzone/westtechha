Family Description
Compact, flexible, and easy to place

The Scout Family is our most compact enclosure option, designed for smaller automation ideas and installs where space is limited. It is a strong fit for DIY projects, compact control applications, and customers who want a clean enclosure they can use almost anywhere they have power.

Printed in PLA+ 2.0. Intended for indoor use only. Not IP-rated and not designed for wet locations.
