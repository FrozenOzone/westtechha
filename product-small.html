Why Small
Why this size exists

The Small kit is for customers who want the cleanest, simplest enclosure option in the lineup without losing the hardware and mounting features that make the product useful.

Best Fit

Compact installs

Best when a smaller footprint matters, wiring is manageable, and you still want a more finished result than a generic project box.

Product Direction

Physical platform first

WestTechHA sells the Small kit as a real enclosure platform, not as a bundled firmware or electronics support package. That keeps the product flexible and avoids charging customers for things they may not need.
