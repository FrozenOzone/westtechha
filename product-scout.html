Family Description
Compact, flexible, and easy to place

The Scout Product Family is our most compact enclosure option, designed for smaller automation ideas and installs where space is limited. It is a strong fit for DIY projects, compact control applications, and customers who want a clean enclosure they can use almost anywhere they have power.

Printed in PLA+ 2.0. Intended for indoor use only. Not IP-rated and not designed for wet or direct sunlight locations.

Material & Compatibility

Built for indoor use and compatible breakout-board layouts

The Scout family is designed around compatible breakout-board layouts and a cleaner physical install path. It is printed in PLA+ 2.0 for indoor use only and is not IP-rated or intended for wet or direct sunlight locations.
