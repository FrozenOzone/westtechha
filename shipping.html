Shipping Policy

Shipping that stays practical.

WestTechHA ships in a practical, cost-conscious way designed for small products, enclosure shells, kits, and quote-based custom work.

Current Policy
Overview

Orders are shipped using a tracked service selected for size, weight, destination, and overall best value.

Order Processing

Most standard orders are processed within a normal small-business handling window unless otherwise noted. Custom or quote-based work may require more lead time.

Shipping Method

Carrier choice may vary by package and destination. Small orders often ship by tracked USPS service or a comparable carrier when appropriate.

Packaging

Products are packed to reduce movement and help prevent damage using corrugated boxes, protective fill, and bagged hardware as needed.

Tracking, Insurance & Signature

Higher value orders may receive extra protection.

Tracking is standard. For higher-value orders, WestTechHA may add insurance or signature confirmation when it makes sense for the order value, replacement cost, or delivery risk.

Problems in Transit

Contact us quickly if something is wrong.

  • General shipping contact: contact@westtechha.com
  • Order-specific issues: orders@westtechha.com
  • Include order details and photos if there is visible package damage.
