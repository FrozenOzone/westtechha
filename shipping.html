SHIPPING • DELIVERY POLICY

WestTech Home Automation Shipping Policy

Effective Date: March 25, 2026

Overview
Read First

We ship orders in a practical, secure, and cost-conscious way. Our goal is to make sure your order is packed well, shipped with tracking, and arrives in good condition.

Most small product orders ship in boxed protective packaging using a tracked service selected for size, weight, destination, and overall best value.

Jump to: Processing · Methods · Tracking · Packaging · Insurance · Addresses · Delivery Times · Lost / Delayed · Transit Damage · Domestic Only

1) Order Processing
Timing

Orders are typically processed within 1–3 business days unless otherwise stated on the product listing, order page, or invoice.

Custom-built, built-to-order, or limited-run items may require additional handling time. If an order needs more time, we will communicate that as clearly as possible.

2) Shipping Methods
Carrier

Shipping method is selected based on package size, package weight, destination, and overall best practical value.

Most small product orders ship using a tracked service such as USPS Ground Advantage or a comparable carrier/service when appropriate.

Unless specifically arranged in advance, WestTech Home Automation does not guarantee the use of a particular carrier or delivery speed.

3) Tracking
Included

All orders include tracking.

Tracking information will be provided once the shipping label has been created and the package has been prepared for carrier pickup or drop-off.

4) Packaging
Protection

Products are shipped in protective packaging designed to reduce movement and help prevent shipping damage.

Depending on the item, packaging may include:

  • corrugated shipping boxes
  • bubble wrap
  • protective fill
  • bagged small hardware or accessories
5) Insurance and Signature Confirmation
Higher Value

For higher-value shipments, WestTech Home Automation may add shipping insurance or signature confirmation.

This is done at our discretion based on order value, replacement cost, destination, and shipment risk.

6) Shipping Addresses
Accuracy

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate shipping address at checkout.

WestTech Home Automation is not responsible for delays, losses, or additional charges caused by incorrect addresses, incomplete addresses, undeliverable locations, or address changes requested after shipment.

If an order is returned because of an address issue, additional shipping charges may apply before reshipment.

7) Delivery Times
Estimates

Delivery timeframes shown by carriers are estimates only and are not guaranteed unless a guaranteed shipping service was specifically purchased.

Once a package has been accepted by the carrier, delivery speed and final delivery handling are outside the direct control of WestTech Home Automation.

8) Lost, Delayed, or Stolen Packages
Transit Issues

If a package is delayed, marked delivered but not received, or appears lost in transit, please contact us as soon as possible.

When contacting us, please include:

  • your order number
  • the shipping name and address used for the order
  • any relevant delivery notes or tracking details

We may assist by confirming shipment details, providing tracking information, and helping start a carrier claim when applicable.

WestTech Home Automation is not responsible for theft after confirmed delivery, but we will make a reasonable effort to help document the issue.

9) Damaged in Transit
Arrival

If your order arrives damaged in transit:

  • report it within 7 days of delivery
  • provide photos of the shipping box, packing materials, and the item
  • include your order number and a short description of the issue

This helps us evaluate the issue and determine the next step as quickly as possible.

10) Domestic Shipping Only
Current Scope

At this time, WestTech Home Automation ships within the United States only unless otherwise stated.

11) Contact
Questions

For shipping, delivery, or order-related questions, contact contact@westtechha.com.

For order-specific issues, you may also contact orders@westtechha.com.
