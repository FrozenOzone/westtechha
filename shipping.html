SHIPPING POLICY

Shipping Policy

WestTechHA ships in a practical, cost-conscious way designed for small products, enclosure shells, kits, and quote-based custom work. This page explains the current shipping approach.

Overview
Current Policy

Orders are shipped using a tracked service selected for size, weight, destination, and overall best value. WestTechHA currently keeps shipping straightforward rather than overcomplicating it with too many service promises.

1) Order Processing
Timing

Most standard orders are processed within a normal small-business handling window unless otherwise noted. Custom, made-to-order, or quote-based work may require more lead time depending on the project.

2) Shipping Method
Tracked Service

Shipping method is selected based on package size, package weight, destination, and best practical value. Small orders will often ship through a tracked USPS service or a comparable carrier/service when appropriate.

  • Tracking is included with normal shipments.
  • Carrier choice may vary by package and destination.
  • A specific carrier is not guaranteed unless arranged in advance.
3) Packaging
Protection

Products are packed to reduce movement and help prevent shipping damage. Depending on the item, this may include:

  • Corrugated shipping boxes
  • Bubble wrap or protective fill
  • Bagged hardware or small accessories
  • Additional protection for fragile printed parts or accessories
4) Tracking, Insurance & Signature
Higher Value Orders

Tracking is standard. For higher-value orders, WestTechHA may add extra insurance or signature confirmation when it makes sense for the order value, replacement cost, or delivery risk.

5) Delivery & Address Accuracy
Important

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate shipping address. Carrier delivery estimates are not guaranteed unless a guaranteed service was specifically arranged.

  • Incorrect or incomplete addresses may delay delivery.
  • Returned packages may require additional shipping charges before reshipment.
  • Once accepted by the carrier, final delivery timing is outside direct WestTechHA control.
6) Problems in Transit
Contact Us

If a package appears delayed, arrives damaged, or shows delivered when you did not receive it, contact us as soon as possible so we can help review the issue.

  • General shipping contact: contact@westtechha.com
  • Order-specific issues: orders@westtechha.com
  • Include order details and photos if there is visible package damage.
