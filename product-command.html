Command Family

The largest enclosure family in the WestTechHA line, built for projects that need the broadest application flexibility, including direct 5V and 12V use where the design calls for it.

Family Overview
Built for the most capable projects in the lineup

Command builds on the Scout and Ranger families and goes a step further for projects that need more room, more flexibility, and a wider application range. It is designed for customers who already know they need more than a compact or mid-size enclosure path.

Application Fit
When Command makes sense

Command is the right family when the project needs direct 5V and 12V use, a larger hardware plan, or the extra flexibility that comes with the broadest enclosure option in the WestTechHA line.

Who It Serves
Installers and advanced DIY customers

Command is a strong fit for customers planning more involved indoor automation work, repeatable installer projects, or builds that have clearly outgrown Scout and Ranger.

Material & Compatibility
Important application notes

Command follows the same material direction and compatibility approach as the rest of the family line.

Material
PLA+ 2.0

The Command family is printed in PLA+ 2.0 and is intended for indoor use only.

Use Limitation
Not IP-rated

Like the rest of the line, Command is not IP-rated and is not intended for wet locations. Customers should confirm the environment is appropriate before ordering.
