Family Description
The largest family in the roadmap

The Command Product Family is planned for larger indoor automation projects where Scout and Ranger do not provide enough room. It remains part of the WestTechHA family structure, but it is intentionally being held back until the design is cleaner.

Printed product direction remains PLA+ 2.0. Intended for indoor use only. These enclosures are not IP-rated and are not designed for wet or direct sunlight locations.
