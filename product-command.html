Command is still intended to be the broadest enclosure family in the WestTechHA line, including the extra room and direct 5V / 12V application direction that set it apart from Scout and Ranger.

Family Description
The broadest application range in the lineup

The Command Family builds on Scout and Ranger and adds the most room and flexibility in the lineup. It is the best choice for larger low-voltage and automation applications, especially where direct 5V and 12V use may be part of the build.

Printed in PLA+ 2.0. Intended for indoor use only. Not IP-rated and not designed for wet locations.

Why Command Still Matters
Why this family remains on the roadmap

Command remains on the roadmap because there is still a real need for a larger family option beyond Scout and Ranger. The redesign is about getting that release right, not walking away from it.

Best Fit

Expanded indoor applications

Best when the project benefits from a larger enclosure family, more internal flexibility, or a more capable hardware plan than Scout or Ranger are intended to handle.

Buying Direction

Built for more involved projects

Command Ready is for customers who already know the hardware path. Command Loaded is there when you want WestTechHA to prepare the family around the appropriate hardware before it arrives.

Material & Compatibility
Important application notes

Command follows the same material direction and compatibility approach as the rest of the family line.

Material
PLA+ 2.0

The Command family is printed in PLA+ 2.0 and is intended for indoor use only.

Use Limitation
Not IP-rated

Like the rest of the line, Command is not IP-rated and is not intended for wet locations. Customers should confirm the environment is appropriate before ordering.
