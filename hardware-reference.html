Board Layout & Hardware Reference

A shared reference for board layout photos, HSI mounting examples, and important notes about hardware shown in WestTechHA product photos.

Important
Hardware photos are for planning unless stated otherwise

Photos may show electronics, wiring, relay modules, buck converters, display modules, buzzers, DHT11 sensors, or other accessories as layout references. Hardware is only included when the selected product option or checkout page specifically states that it is included.

Unloaded Products
Bring-your-own-hardware path

Unloaded products are enclosure-focused. They include the printed enclosure path and stated enclosure hardware, but electronics and firmware are not included unless specifically stated.

Loaded Products
Pre-assembled path where listed

Loaded products include the selected electronics path where listed, plus pre-shipment diagnostics and Quick Start firmware for ESP32-based products.
