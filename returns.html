Returns • Limited Warranty

Clearer returns and warranty handling.

This page explains how WestTechHA currently handles damaged deliveries, return requests, and limited product warranty issues for enclosure shells, kits, and approved custom work.

Policy
Overview

WestTechHA is a small operation, so returns and warranty handling are kept straightforward.

  • Report shipping damage or missing items as soon as possible.
  • Do not modify or drill a product first and then request a return for fitment reasons.
  • Custom and made-to-order work may have different return limits than standard items.
Shipping Damage / DOA

If an order arrives damaged, incomplete, or clearly unusable on arrival, contact us as soon as possible and include clear photos of the package and item.

Standard Returns

Standard returns are handled case by case. Unused standard items are the easiest to review for return eligibility.

Custom Work

Custom, modified, or made-to-order items are generally not treated the same way as standard stock items and may not be returnable unless there is a confirmed defect.

Limited Warranty

What it generally covers

WestTechHA’s limited warranty is meant to cover clear defects in the product itself, not misuse, accidental damage, poor installation, or changes made after delivery.

  • Coverage generally focuses on confirmed production defects
  • It does not cover incorrect installation or abuse
  • Normal wear and customer modifications are not treated as warranty defects
