Returns & Limited Warranty

This page explains how WestTechHA currently handles damaged deliveries, return requests, and limited product warranty issues for enclosure shells, kits, and approved custom work.

Overview
Policy

WestTechHA is a small operation, so returns and warranty handling are kept straightforward. If there is a problem, the fastest path is to contact support quickly with clear photos and order details.

  • Report shipping damage or missing items as soon as possible.
  • Do not modify or drill a product first and then request a return for fitment reasons.
  • Custom and made-to-order work may have different return limits than standard items.
1) Shipping Damage / DOA
Report Fast

If an order arrives damaged, incomplete, or clearly unusable on arrival, contact us as soon as possible.

  • Include your order name or order number if available.
  • Include clear photos of the outer package, inner packaging, and the item itself.
  • Keep all original packaging until the issue is resolved.

The faster you contact us, the easier it is to verify carrier damage and handle the issue cleanly.

2) Standard Returns
Case by Case

Standard returns are handled case by case. Because many products involve configurable printed parts, fitment choices, and limited-run production, not every item is automatically returnable like a mass-market item.

  • Unused standard items are the easiest to review for return eligibility.
  • Customer-modified items may not be eligible for return.
  • Opened kits may be handled differently than unopened standard items.
3) Custom / Made-to-Order Work
Important

Custom, modified, or made-to-order items are generally not treated the same way as standard stock items. If a project was built to a specific request, that work may not be returnable unless there is a confirmed defect or a verified build mistake on our side.

  • Custom cutouts, custom layouts, and quote-based work should be reviewed carefully before approval.
  • Questions are always better before production than after production.
  • If something is genuinely wrong, reach out and we will review it fairly.
4) Limited Warranty
What It Means

WestTechHA’s limited warranty is meant to cover clear defects in the product itself, not misuse, accidental damage, poor installation practices, or changes made after delivery.

  • Coverage generally focuses on product defects and confirmed production issues.
  • It does not cover damage from incorrect installation, over-tightening, abuse, or unrelated project failures.
  • Normal wear, cosmetic variation in printed parts, and customer modifications are not treated as warranty defects.
5) Contact for Returns or Warranty
Support

The best way to start a return or warranty conversation is support email.

  • Support: support@westtechha.com
  • General contact: contact@westtechha.com
  • Include photos, what happened, and what product you received.
