Returns • Limited Warranty

Clear returns and warranty handling.

This page explains how WestTechHA handles shipping damage, return requests, and limited warranty questions for enclosure families, Ready or Loaded orders, included hardware, and approved special-request items.

Policy
Overview

WestTechHA is a small operation, so returns and warranty handling are kept straightforward and product-focused.

  • Report shipping damage, missing items, or obvious defects as soon as possible.
  • Do not modify, drill, cut, or alter a product before requesting a return for fitment reasons.
  • Standard family products and Ready / Loaded orders are reviewed differently from special-request or made-to-order items.
Shipping Damage / Missing Items

If an order arrives damaged, incomplete, or unusable on arrival, contact WestTechHA promptly and include clear photos of the package, label, and product.

Standard Returns

Unused standard products are the easiest items to review for return eligibility. Return handling is still reviewed case by case so product condition can be confirmed first.

Special-Request Items

Special-request, modified, or made-to-order items are not treated the same way as standard stock items and may not be returnable unless there is a confirmed defect.

Limited Warranty

What it generally covers

WestTechHA’s limited warranty is intended to cover clear defects in the product itself, not misuse, accidental damage, poor installation, or changes made after delivery.

  • Coverage focuses on confirmed production defects
  • It does not cover incorrect installation, abuse, or accidental damage
  • Normal wear and customer modifications are not treated as warranty defects
