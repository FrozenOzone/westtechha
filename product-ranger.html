Family Description
More room without jumping to the largest family

The Ranger Product Family is the balanced middle option in the WestTechHA enclosure line. It is designed for projects that need more space than Scout, including relay-oriented applications and layouts that benefit from extra internal room.

Printed in PLA+ 2.0. Intended for indoor use only. Not IP-rated and not designed for wet or direct sunlight locations.

Material & Compatibility

Built for indoor use and compatible breakout-board layouts

The Ranger family is designed around compatible breakout-board layouts and a cleaner physical install path. It is printed in PLA+ 2.0 for indoor use only and is not IP-rated or intended for wet or direct sunlight locations.
