Description & Dimensions
What Ranger is and how big it is

Ranger is the balanced middle family. It keeps the product line simple while giving customers two clear layout choices inside the same family.

Family Role
More room than Scout

Ranger is built for projects that need more internal room, cleaner wiring space, or a larger working footprint than Scout while still staying in a practical indoor enclosure size.
