WESTTECH COASTER COLOR TOOLS
============================

This folder owns the five internal coaster color tools and their tool-only assets.

1. sports-color-lab.html
   Single Team colors, logo sizing, and approval/export. NFL Team Logo and WestTech Team Art use independent saved profiles/baselines.

2. house-divided-color-lab.html
   House Divided field/logo review and production profile export.

3. house-accent-review.html
   Approved Preferred/Alternate house accents and collision review.

Tool-only CSS/JS lives under assets/. Shared production baselines and customer-builder code remain outside this folder because the live customer pages also use them.

Keep permanent production workflows here; put temporary experiments under _local-notes instead of adding throwaway lab pages.

4. blackout-color-lab.html
   NFL WestTech Team Art Blackout review: fixed Black fields/center, per-team separator + logo outline + size, approved house accents, independent JS export.

5. single-blackout-color-lab.html
   NFL WestTech Team Art Single Team Blackout review. Black field is fixed; ring colors, whole-logo outline, and full-size logo fit are approved independently. Exports nfl-team-art-single-blackout-baseline.js.
