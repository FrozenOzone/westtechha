Why Customers Like This Structure

Clear products. Clear buying path.

Instead of forcing customers through confusing kit language or one-off product names, WestTechHA uses one simple path: choose the family, then choose Unloaded or Loaded. That makes it easier to quote, easier to support, and easier to buy with confidence.

Material Note

Printed in PLA+ 2.0 for indoor use.

WestTechHA enclosure families are printed in PLA+ 2.0. They are intended for indoor use only and are not IP-rated or designed for wet or direct sunlight locations. Customers should make sure the intended application matches that material limitation.
