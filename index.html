New to ESP32 Projects?
Small controller. Big home automation potential.

An ESP32 is a small Wi-Fi enabled controller used in many DIY smart-home and automation projects. With the right sensors, wiring, and firmware, it can monitor doors, motion, temperature, humidity, buttons, relays, and other low-voltage devices.

Material Note

Printed in PLA+ 2.0 for indoor use.

WestTechHA enclosure families are printed in PLA+ 2.0. They are intended for indoor use only and are not IP-rated or designed for wet or direct sunlight locations. Customers should make sure the intended application matches that material limitation.
