Why This Direction
Current Focus

Enclosures are the most flexible place to start. They work for DIYers, hobbyists, installers, and custom one-off projects without forcing every customer into the same full-build product.

  • Cleaner than generic project boxes
  • Easier to ship, support, and iterate
  • Useful even when the customer brings their own hardware
  • A better public starting point than a narrow single-use controller

WestTechHA still supports the deeper ESP32/custom-build side of the business, but that now sits behind the enclosure line instead of in front of it. That keeps the catalog clearer and makes it easier to grow without promising too much too early.
