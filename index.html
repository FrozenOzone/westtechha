Family Overview
Three families built around real project needs

Each family is meant to be easy to understand, easy to compare, and flexible enough to serve both experienced DIY builds and repeatable installer work.

Compact Family
Scout
Small footprint • flexible indoor use

Scout is the compact starting point. It is small enough to use almost anywhere you have power and is a strong fit for DIY projects, clean retrofits, and smaller automation ideas.

  • Smallest family in the line
  • Available in Unloaded or Loaded
  • Compatible 30-pin and 38-pin layouts

Balanced Family
Ranger
More room • more flexibility

Ranger builds on Scout by giving you more internal room and more flexibility, including projects that benefit from relay-oriented layouts or a little more working space.

  • Middle-ground family
  • Available in Unloaded or Loaded
  • Great fit when Scout feels tight

Why Customers Like This Structure

Clear products. Clear buying path.

Instead of forcing customers through confusing kit language or one-off product names, WestTechHA uses one simple path: choose the family, then choose Unloaded or Loaded. That makes it easier to quote, easier to support, and easier to buy with confidence.

Material Note

Printed in PLA+ 2.0 for indoor use.

WestTechHA enclosure families are printed in PLA+ 2.0. They are intended for indoor use only and are not IP-rated or designed for wet or direct sunlight locations. Customers should make sure the intended application matches that material limitation.
