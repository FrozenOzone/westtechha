How to Start

Step 1
Choose a Family

Select Scout, Ranger, or Command based on the size, flexibility, and application needs of the project.

Step 2
Choose Ready or Loaded

Ready means the enclosure is ready for your hardware. Loaded means it is pre-loaded with the hardware appropriate for the selected family.

Step 3
Request Help if Needed

Need fit guidance, layout review, or a custom direction? WestTechHA can help you sort that out before you buy.

Family Overview
Three families built around real project needs

Each family is meant to be easy to understand, easy to compare, and flexible enough to serve both experienced DIY builds and repeatable installer work.

Compact Family
Scout
Small footprint • flexible indoor use

Scout is the compact starting point. It is small enough to use almost anywhere you have power and is a strong fit for DIY projects, clean retrofits, and smaller automation ideas.

  • Smallest family in the line
  • Available in Ready or Loaded
  • Compatible 30-pin and 38-pin layouts

Balanced Family
Ranger
More room • more flexibility

Ranger builds on Scout by giving you more internal room and more flexibility, including projects that benefit from relay-oriented layouts or a little more working space.

  • Middle-ground family
  • Available in Ready or Loaded
  • Great fit when Scout feels tight

Expanded Family
Command
Broader application range

Command builds on Scout and Ranger and goes a step further with direct 5V and 12V use for projects that need the broadest application flexibility in the family line.

  • Largest family
  • Available in Ready or Loaded
  • Built for expanded indoor applications

Material Note

Printed in PLA+ 2.0 for indoor use.

WestTechHA enclosure families are printed in PLA+ 2.0. They are intended for indoor use only and are not IP-rated or designed for wet locations. Customers should make sure the intended application matches that material limitation.
