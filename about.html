Our Mission

WestTech Home Automation is a veteran-owned company focused on building enclosure systems and related project support for ESP32 and home automation work that real people can understand, use, and maintain.

The mission is not to overpromise. It is to offer cleaner hardware paths, honest product direction, and practical support for DIYers, installers, and custom one-off projects.

Our Story

WestTech Home Automation started as a veteran’s effort to build the kind of smart-home gear he actually wanted to use himself: local, understandable, and not dependent on disposable cloud gadgets.

That work naturally involved ESP32 boards, Home Assistant, Ethernet modules, wiring plans, and 3D-printed enclosures. Over time, one thing became very clear: a good enclosure solves a lot of problems for a lot more people than a narrow single-purpose controller.

That is why the current business direction is enclosure-first. Custom ESP32 builds are still part of the business, but they now sit behind a cleaner public product line built around shells, kits, and quote-based project work.

Why This Direction Makes Sense
Cleaner Public Catalog

Enclosures and kits are easier for customers to understand and easier for WestTechHA to present clearly. That makes the product line more honest and easier to grow.

Useful to More People

A good enclosure helps DIYers, hobbyists, installers, and custom builders alike. It works whether the customer is bringing their own board or asking for more help.

Custom Builds Still Matter

The deeper ESP32 and automation side of WestTechHA is not gone. It is simply handled in the right place: behind a quote and a conversation instead of being treated like the default product for everyone.

Documentation Still Matters

WestTechHA still believes in clear notes, wiring references, and practical support. Even when the public offering starts with enclosures, the long-term goal is still hardware that is cleaner, supportable, and easier to understand.
