About • WestTech Home Automation

Built around practical enclosure kits.

WestTechHA is a veteran-owned business focused on enclosure kits that make ESP32 and home-automation projects look cleaner, mount better, and stay easier to work on over time.

Our Mission

Build enclosure products that give customers a cleaner, more flexible way to finish compatible electronics projects.

WestTech Home Automation is centered on the physical side of the project: enclosure fit, lid options, mounting hardware, inserts, and practical support. The goal is to give customers a better finished product without forcing them to buy electronics, firmware, or bundled services they may not need.

Our Story

WestTechHA grew out of real project work: ESP32 boards, breakout boards, sensors, wiring, and the constant need for something cleaner than exposed hardware on a bench or in a rough box.

That work made one thing clear very quickly: a well-designed enclosure helps a lot more people than a narrow one-off controller. A good enclosure kit can support hobbyists, repeatable installs, experiments, and special projects alike, as long as the board layout and footprint are compatible.

Why this approach works

WestTechHA stays focused on the part of the project that should be done well every time: the enclosure itself.

Clear Product Line

Small, Med, and Big enclosure kits are easier to understand, easier to compare, and easier to order than a mixed catalog of one-off builds.

Better Physical Value

Hardware, magnets, screws, lids, and matched inserts are included so customers get more useful mounting options right out of the box.

Flexible Compatibility

The kits are designed for compatible breakout-board layouts, giving customers room to bring their own board setup instead of being forced into one exact electronics package.

Honest Scope

WestTechHA keeps the public product line focused on enclosure kits while still leaving room for special requests when a project needs more discussion.
