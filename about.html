About • WestTech Home Automation

Built around practical enclosure families.

WestTechHA is a veteran-owned business focused on Scout, Ranger, and Command enclosure families that help compatible electronics projects look cleaner, mount better, and stay easier to work on over time.

Our Mission

Build enclosure products that give customers a cleaner, more flexible way to finish compatible electronics projects.

WestTech Home Automation is centered on the physical side of the project: enclosure fit, lid options, mounting hardware, inserts, and practical support. The goal is to give customers a better finished product without forcing them into electronics, firmware, or service bundles they may not need.

Our Story

WestTechHA grew out of real project work: ESP32 boards, breakout boards, sensors, wiring, and the constant need for something cleaner than exposed hardware on a bench or in a generic box.

That work made one thing clear quickly: a well-designed enclosure helps more people than a one-off controller ever will. A good enclosure family can serve hobbyists, installers, repeatable projects, and custom ideas alike, as long as the layout and footprint are compatible.

Why this approach works

WestTechHA stays focused on the part of the project that should be done well every time: the enclosure itself.

Clear Product Line

Scout, Ranger, and Command are easier to understand, easier to compare, and easier to order than a mixed catalog of one-off builds.

Better Physical Value

Hardware, magnets, lids, and matched inserts are treated as part of the enclosure value instead of an afterthought.

Flexible Compatibility

The families are designed around compatible breakout-board layouts, giving customers room to bring their own board path instead of being forced into one exact electronics package.

Honest Scope

WestTechHA keeps the public product line focused on enclosure families and Ready / Loaded options while still leaving room for special-request projects when they make sense.
