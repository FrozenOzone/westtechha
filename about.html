Our Story

WestTech Home Automation started as a veteran’s effort to build the kind of smart-home gear he actually wanted to use himself: local, understandable, and not dependent on disposable cloud gadgets.

That work naturally involved ESP32 boards, Home Assistant, Ethernet modules, wiring plans, and 3D-printed enclosures. Over time, one thing became very clear: a good enclosure solves a lot of problems for a lot more people than a narrow controller.
