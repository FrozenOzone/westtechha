How WestTechHA works.

WestTechHA leads with Scout, Ranger, and Command families in Ready or Loaded versions, then supports customers with documentation, guided selection, and special-request quotes when a project needs more than the standard path.

Working Model
Three ways customers typically work with WestTechHA

Simple, practical, and easier to support over time.

Standard Product Path
Ready or Loaded
Best fit for many customers

You already know the project direction and want the family and offer level that fit best. This is the most direct path for many DIY customers and many installer purchases.

  • Best fit for straightforward Ready or Loaded orders
  • Good for hobbyists, installers, and repeatable builds
  • Keeps cost and complexity under control
Guided Selection
Help choosing the right fit
Quote help without overcommitting early

You are still doing the install, but want help choosing the right family, openings, lid style, or buying path before placing an order.

  • Good when the project is still taking shape
  • Useful for compatibility and layout questions
  • Helpful for both installers and DIY customers
Special-Request Work
Project-specific builds
By request

When the project needs selected hardware, custom cutouts, or deeper project discussion, WestTechHA can quote that separately.

  • Handled through quote review
  • For projects needing more than a standard family order
  • Kept separate from the standard buying path
Support Structure

Documentation still matters.

WestTechHA supports customers with wiring references, family-selection help, known-good notes, and practical documentation that makes the physical side of the project easier to understand.

  • Support – product help and common questions
  • Forum – community questions, answers, and ideas
  • Products – Scout, Ranger, Command, Ready, and Loaded
Working With Us

Start with a clear question.

The easiest way to begin is to describe what you are building, what hardware you already have, and whether Scout, Ranger, or Command with Ready or Loaded feels like the right starting point.
