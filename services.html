How Customers Usually Start
DIY • Assisted • Custom
DIY / Bring Your Own Hardware

This is the cleanest fit for many customers. You already know the board or project you want to use, and you simply want a better enclosure path than generic project boxes.

  • Best fit for shell and kit buyers
  • Good for hobbyists, installers, and repeatable one-off projects
  • Keeps cost and complexity lower
Assisted DIY / Quote Help

You are still doing the install, but you want help choosing the enclosure size, thinking through cutouts, or deciding whether a shell, kit, or custom build makes the most sense.

  • Good when the project is still taking shape
  • Useful for custom opening requests and layout questions
  • Lets you get guidance without jumping straight to a full custom build

Full turnkey and on-site services are not the main public direction right now. WestTechHA is intentionally keeping the business cleaner: shells and kits first, then custom quoted work when the project really needs it.
