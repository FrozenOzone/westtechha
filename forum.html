Rules

Community Rules

  • Be respectful and keep replies useful.
  • Do not post private account details, full addresses, or anything sensitive.
  • For safety-related issues, include exact behavior and exact hardware details.
  • When a fix works, post it so the next person can find it.
Before You Post

Helpful details

  • Which family or product you are using
  • Whether your setup is Ready or Loaded
  • Board type or hardware involved
  • What changed
  • What you already tried
  • Any logs, photos, or screenshots
Forum Flow

How to use the forum

  • Browse categories from this page.
  • Use GitHub Discussions for active topics and replies.
  • Use Support for structured product help.
  • Share the working answer when something is solved.
