Community Guidelines
Rules
  • Be respectful and keep replies useful.
  • Do not post Wi-Fi passwords, full addresses, or personal/private account details.
  • If something is safety-critical, include exact behavior and exact hardware details.
  • When a solution works, post the fix so the next person can find it.
