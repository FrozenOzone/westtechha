Why Ranger
Why this family exists

Ranger gives customers a stronger middle-ground option: more interior room, more flexibility, and a better fit for builds that outgrow Scout without moving all the way up to Command.

Best Fit

Material & Compatibility

Built for indoor use and compatible breakout-board layouts

The Ranger family is designed around compatible breakout-board layouts and a cleaner physical install path. It is printed in PLA+ 2.0 for indoor use only and is not IP-rated or intended for wet locations.
