Common Questions

Questions customers commonly ask

  • “Where should I start?”
    Use Enclosures for Scout, Ranger, Command, and ESP32-specific information. Use Coasters for coaster collections and product details.
  • “Can I ask a product question before ordering?”
    Yes. Use Support or Contact when you want an option, fit, availability, or ordering question checked first.
  • “Do you offer the STL files separately?”
    STL files are not offered at this time. WestTech Home Automation is currently focused on finished physical products so fit, finish, and overall quality stay consistent.
  • “What if my order does not fit the standard purchase path?”
    Use the Quote page when quantity, product details, or another order question needs review before purchase.
