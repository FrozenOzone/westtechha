Why This Way
Why WestTechHA sells the product this way

WestTechHA includes the enclosure hardware and physical options that should come standard with the product, while keeping electronics, firmware, and code support out of the default kit unless a project actually needs that level of work.

What You Get
A more complete enclosure kit

The product is centered on the physical platform: better mounting options, cleaner inserts, installed hardware, and a more finished enclosure path from day one.

  • More useful out of the box
  • Less piecing parts together later
  • Cleaner fit for DIY and installer projects
