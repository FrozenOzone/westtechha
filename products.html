Three enclosure families. Two buying options.

WestTechHA organizes the product line into Scout, Ranger, and Command families. Scout and Ranger are the active families today. Command remains part of the roadmap and is currently marked Coming Soon while its layout is being redesigned.

Product Families

Offer Types
Unloaded vs. Loaded

The family tells you which enclosure path fits the project. Unloaded or Loaded tells you how much of the hardware path you want WestTechHA to handle before it arrives.

Unloaded
Unloaded for your hardware plan

Unloaded is built for the customer who already knows the hardware path and simply needs the enclosure family that fits the project.

  • Best for bring-your-own-hardware projects
  • Good fit for experienced DIY and installer work
  • Cleaner enclosure path without unnecessary hardware bundling
Loaded
Pre-loaded for the selected family

Loaded is exactly what it sounds like: the enclosure is pre-loaded with the hardware appropriate for the selected family. The active standard family paths are Scout and Ranger while Command remains coming soon.

  • Best when you want more handled up front
  • Hardware package follows the selected family
  • Quoted based on family and application needs
