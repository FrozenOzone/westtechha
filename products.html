Products • Scout • Ranger • Command

Three enclosure families. Two buying options.

WestTechHA organizes the product line into Scout, Ranger, and Command families. Scout and Ranger are the active families today. Command remains part of the roadmap but is currently marked Coming Soon while its layout is being redesigned.

Family Line
Choose the family that fits the application

Each family is built around the same idea: a cleaner, better-finished enclosure for compatible breakout-board layouts. What changes is the amount of room and application flexibility the project needs.

Scout Family
Compact and flexible
Small footprint • strong DIY and light install fit

Scout is the smallest family in the line and the easiest place to start when you need a compact indoor enclosure for a clean, practical project build.

  • Best for smaller indoor projects
  • Available as Ready or Loaded
  • Compatible 30-pin and 38-pin layouts
  • Approx. size: 3.15" × 4.09" × 1.77" to 3.54" × 4.09" × 1.77"

Offer Types
Ready vs. Loaded

The family tells you which enclosure path fits the project. Ready or Loaded tells you how much of the hardware path you want WestTechHA to handle before it arrives.

Ready
Ready for your hardware

Ready is built for the customer who already knows the hardware path and simply needs the enclosure family that fits the project.

  • Best for bring-your-own-hardware projects
  • Good fit for experienced DIY and installer work
  • Cleaner enclosure path without unnecessary hardware bundling
Loaded
Pre-loaded for the selected family

Loaded is exactly what it sounds like: the enclosure is pre-loaded with the hardware appropriate for the selected family. At the moment, the active standard family paths are Scout and Ranger while Command remains coming soon.

  • Best when you want more handled up front
  • Hardware package follows the selected family
  • Quoted based on family and application needs
Material Note
