Three enclosure families. Two buying options.

WestTechHA organizes the product line into Scout, Ranger, and Command families. Each family can be compared by enclosure size, project fit, and whether you want the product Unloaded or Loaded.

Offer Types
Unloaded vs. Loaded

The family tells you which enclosure path fits the project. Unloaded or Loaded tells you how much of the hardware path you want WestTechHA to handle before it arrives.

Unloaded
Unloaded for your hardware plan

Unloaded is the bring-your-own-hardware path for customers who already know their electronics plan and simply need the enclosure family that fits the project.

  • Includes the printed enclosure, lid, brass inserts, and standard enclosure hardware
  • Best for experienced DIY and installer work
  • Electronics and firmware are not included unless specifically stated at checkout
Loaded
Pre-loaded with Quick Start firmware

Loaded is the pre-assembled path for customers who want the selected electronics path handled before shipment. Loaded ESP32-based products include a basic pre-shipment diagnostic check and WestTech Home Automation ESP32 Quick Start firmware.

  • Best when you want more handled up front
  • Basic diagnostics are completed before the order ships
  • Quick Start firmware helps you find, connect, verify, and prepare the device for your own firmware
Loaded setup

Diagnostics completed before shipment

Loaded ESP32-based products are not shipped as blank boards. WestTech Home Automation performs a basic pre-shipment diagnostic check and installs ESP32 Quick Start firmware so customers have a simple path to power on, connect, verify, and then upload their own project firmware.
